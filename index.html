WELCOME TO JO'S BAKERY

Freshly Baked Happiness Every Day

At Jo's Bakery, we believe every day should start with the smell of fresh bread. Baked daily from 4am with locally sourced ingredients and family recipes.

WHO WE ARE

Jo's Bakery is a family-owned bakery based in Pretoria. Founded in 2020 by Jo Nkosi, what started as a small kitchen baking for neighbours during lockdown has grown into a trusted local bakery.
