ABOUT JO'S BAKERY

OUR STORY

Founded in 2020 in Pretoria, Jo's Bakery started with one goal: to bring real, homemade bakery taste back to our community. What began in a small home kitchen during lockdown has grown into a trusted bakery.

OUR MISSION

To deliver top-tier baked goods while building a community of people who love good food.

OUR VISION

To be the #1 go-to bakery in South Africa, known for innovation, authentic taste, quality ingredients, and outstanding customer service.
